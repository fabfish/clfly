- **Two rungs and not a sweep**: the clause carries the two- and three-trial splits, and the **four- and six-trial

- **And one placement pair**: the label is first or last here, and the passes with the label at an **interior trial**

- **And a meter and not a game**: the runs the clause is read from carry **no reward on the action**, and the payout

  right, and the rung's own numbers are `e497`'s reading rather than an independent draw.
- **And one world**: two runs at one budget, one episode length and one seed stream, so a clause that grows is still a
  record of one configuration and a redraw would have to beat the corpus's own arm spread.

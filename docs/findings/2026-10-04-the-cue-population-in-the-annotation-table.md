question `e400` asked geometrically. Five properties of the six cue populations whose far-point readings exist are
read off the annotation table -- the repository's own reason for asking continual-learning questions of a connectome
rather than of a random graph -- and **nothing is trained**. Five claims, registered before any annotation was read.*

## 2. What the screen says

**The card's cue population is not biologically distinctive.** It carries **12 distinct cell types**, 6 cell classes,
11 hemilineages and 12 supertypes -- nearly maximally diverse for twelve neurons -- and **two of the five failing
populations are exactly as diverse** (cue seed 9 and cue seed 1 at 12 cell types and 12 supertypes each), with a
class concentration of 0.417 that cue seed 1 and cue seed 3 share. On every one of the five properties its value
lies inside the range the five failing populations span, and none of the five is monotone in the six readings.

**So the screen is now empty on three kinds of property.** `e397` and `e398` closed the cue's **distance** to the
action population; `e400` screened five **geometric** properties and `e401` retired the one that looked like a lead;
`e402` closed the trained bodies' **movement and alignment**; and this closes the **annotation table**, which is the
substrate's own biological structure. What is left unmeasured is the cue's weighted spectrum, the action
population's own tree, the table's other columns, and -- the direction that now looks strongest -- the **training**
itself, since `e396` measured that the draw is invisible before the weights move and a third of the reading after.

**And the invariance holds once more.** The six populations' connectome readings are 0.6875 on all six, as they have
been on every world this line has measured.

## 3. What it cannot settle, and what it registers

- **Five properties are five of many**: the table's other columns (`flow`, `side`, `super_class`), the weighted
  spectrum of the cue's block and the action population's own tree are unmeasured.
- **And it is a six-population screen with one positive**: the direction registered was the prior and not a finding,
  and a property that separated them could do so by chance.
- **And the properties are of the cue population alone**: the action and feedback populations and the world's three
  maps are held at the card's, so a difference that needs them is outside this screen -- which is exactly what
  `e403` found the far point's difference to be, since a world's streams replicate where its peers' worlds do not.
- *And a probe is not a mechanism.*

# The Claims section now states what each claim is measured at — three questions instead of a status

**Date:** 2026-09-25
**A consolidation unit: no new measurements, and the plan is the artifact.** Today produced eleven findings that change
how the claims below them should be *read* — two draws measured, a corpus-wide census of which families claim at one
draw, an audit showing no declared comparison is confounded, and a replication that took four of the overlap axis's
six claims apart. None of that was visible to a reader who opened `docs/research_plan.md` at its **Claims** section,
which is where a reader starts. This unit puts it there.

---

## 1. What was added, and where

**A box at the head of `## Claims`, before the first claim**, stating:

- **the two draws and their measured sizes** — the support draw moves the learning quantities by 3.3–6.6σ at
  identical configurations and seeds; the read-out draw is unresolved on all twelve quantity-by-end combinations at
  the size this project uses (32) and *is* resolved on the distant-pair interference at size 300, so its smallness is
  size-specific; and **the draw cancels in comparisons between arms at the same supports**, which is why the λ = 1.0
  family's 12.3σ saturation is unaffected while every cross-overlap claim is not;
- **the replication's tally** — of the four registered claims on the overlap axis, at three support draws, **exactly
  one survives** (the endpoint's forgetting rise, 2.99σ / 3.30σ / 2.70σ, and 4.82σ at a second read-out draw), and it
  is the claim the project already had as *nine of nine matched comparisons*;
- **the corpus-wide exposure** — 41 of 59 families, 215 of 285 artifacts, making manipulation claims at a single
  draw, with **no declared comparison confounded** (27 of 27 same draw).

**And a closing sentence that is the unit's only new instruction**: each claim should be read with three questions —
*which family carries it*, *how many draws that family used*, and *whether the draw's magnitude has been measured for
that manipulation*. That replaces a status with a procedure, which is what the day's evidence supports: the same
claim can be well-measured at one draw and unstable at the next, and the two are indistinguishable from the numbers
alone.

**Two claim-level notes** where today's findings bear directly:

- **C2** (the core contribution) — the rung tables are single-draw families, stated at the claim rather than only in a
  census; that a rung table at one draw is not automatically safe (the draw's measured size), that the ladder's design
  makes a second draw a re-run rather than a new instrument, and that the rung rows' own draw variations in
  `e113`/`e117` are read-out draws, which are the small ones at these sizes.
- **C4** (the overlap axis) — a verbatim verdict block with all six judgments, the replication's tally, the two
  headline results named as single-draw quantities, the x-axis's arithmetic correction, and what a quotable shape
  claim would cost (five levels × three or more support draws) and why it was not launched (the two extra interior
  levels were stopped after 1 of 40 replicates, before any artifact was written, once R2's falsifier fired).

## 2. Why a consolidation is a unit and not bookkeeping

**The day's findings are individually correct and collectively invisible.** A reader arriving at the Claims section
sees C1–C4 with status paragraphs written before any of this, and the only way to learn that C4's headline is a
one-draw quantity is to read eleven findings in date order. That is the same defect the day kept finding in other
forms — **a fact that is in the corpus but not where it is needed** — and it has a documentation shape: the status
paragraphs are *per-claim* while the day's lesson is *per-claim and per-draw*, so the misses were structural.

**And the consolidation is honest about what it does not settle.** It states that the axis's shape question is
unanswered rather than answered negatively; that the 41 single-draw families are a prompt to measure *that family's*
draw rather than a defect list; and that the support draw's magnitude is measured on one family's quantities and not
transferable to another's.

## 3. What this does not do

- **It changes no number and re-reads no artifact.** Every figure in the added text is quoted from a finding listed
  beside it, and `e192` (the plan's citations against the findings corpus) and `e105` (the findings' own tables) pass
  unchanged.
- **It does not make the claims weaker than the evidence.** The claims' own sections are untouched except where a
  finding directly contradicts a sentence in them; the box is about *how to read*, not about withdrawing C1–C4.
- **It does not settle which of the 41 single-draw families are at risk.** The draw's magnitude is per-manipulation
  and measured for two manipulations only; the three questions are the procedure for finding out, not an answer.

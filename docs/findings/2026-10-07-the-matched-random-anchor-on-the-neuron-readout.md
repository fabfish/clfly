## 2. What the second anchor says

**`e455`'s prediction held for one arm and not for the other, and the difference is the unit's result.** On the neuron
read-out the matched-random arm's newest-task cost is **-0.0323** at **2.77** sigma -- it **resolves** -- while the
biological arm's is **-0.0115** at **0.78** and does not. So the sentence *the anchor's price is the earned label's and
not the anchoring's* is true of `ewc-block` and false of `ewc-block-rand`: the biological arm's price is the head's and
the matched-random arm's is not, and **the two arms' prices, which were 0.0042 apart on the earned label, are 0.0208
apart on the neurons, with which of them pays more reversing** (the biological pays more on the earned label, the
matched-random pays nearly three times more on the neurons). The card's clauses about *the anchor* therefore cannot be
scoped to a read-out any more than they can be read as one arm's, and `e455`'s reading is one arm's reading.

**And the pair's standings agree under both heads while the pair's prices do not.** On the mean diagonal the two anchors
are **0.0083** apart on the neurons against **0.0063** on the earned label -- the same order as the **0.0042** to
**0.0101** the three pair units measured -- and **none of the four reaches two sigma**, so the corpus's basis contrast
is a null on the diagonal under **both** read-outs, at **0.49** and **0.36** sigma, and on forgetting at **0.32** and
**1.24**. The one place in this whole line where the two anchors separate by resolution is the **newest-task price on the
neuron read-out**, which is a corner of the pair's four-cell table and not its diagonals.

**And the same asymmetry is already in the corpus's oldest reading of this pair.** `e439` found on the earned label that
the matched-random arm's *standing* was the one that resolved (**-0.0365** at **2.16** sigma against the biological
arm's **-0.0302** at **1.77**), and here its *price* is the one that resolves. So on this world the matched-random
arm's effects are the larger and the better resolved of the two on the two occasions where either resolves at all --
which is the direction a reader who expected the cell-class basis to matter would not predict, and is consistent with
the eleven nulls the basis contrast has accumulated rather than with a small biological advantage hiding inside them.

**And the buffer is ahead of both anchors on this head, by the same amount.** **+0.1174** at **7.50** sigma over the
matched-random arm against **+0.1090** at **7.66** over the biological one -- **0.0084** apart, which is the same
quantity as the two anchors' own **0.0083** separation read from the other side, and both far above `e455`'s **+0.1090**
against `ewc-block` on the earned label. So the method contrast is the read-out's in size and the anchors' in nothing.

## 3. What it cannot settle

- **One read-out moved, two things changed**: the circuit's **32** neurons differ from the world's **8** both in what
  they read and in width, and `e448`/`e449` measured the width axis on the earned label alone -- so the **0.0208** by
  which the pair's prices separated is not distinguished here from the widening it contains.
- **And one cell**: the card's world at twenty replicates, so the other five draws and the three streams are not in the
  reading, and a price at **2.77** sigma against one at **0.78** is a pair a redraw could reorder.
- **And one partition draw**: the matched-random partition is a draw of the same group sizes and not the family of them,
  so *the matched-random arm pays more here* is a statement about one draw of it.
- **And two arms are not a mechanism**: that the two prices separate on this head and not the other does not say which
  of `e440`'s two parameters moves them -- `e440` and `e442` read those on the earned label and neither on this head.
